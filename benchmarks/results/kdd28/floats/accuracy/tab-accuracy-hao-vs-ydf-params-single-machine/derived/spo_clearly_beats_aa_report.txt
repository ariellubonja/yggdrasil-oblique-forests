  Effect: binary AUC sets use the test-AUC edge SPO - AA. Regression sets use the relative RMSE reduction (AA - SPO) / AA per fold.
  Both are averaged over folds and over the 5 seeds (YDF wide sets have seed 1 only).
  RECOMMENDED RULE: 5-seed mean effect > 0.005 AUC (regression: > 1 % relative RMSE reduction), AND the one-sided paired t-test over the
    seed-averaged fold differences is significant after Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.05 over all 100 dataset rows, AND the effect is > 0 in every seed.
